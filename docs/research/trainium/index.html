Trainium: inference-capacity scenarios

22 October 2026 · Separate research extension. Existing published scores and growth figures are unchanged.

Dedicated roofline projections are materially above the H100-FLOPs fallback. The large scale-up domain explains much of the difference—not superior per-chip compute. Network scenarios bracket unresolved AWS specification conflicts; they are not confidence intervals or measured Neuron performance.
